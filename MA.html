Dati finanziari per: Mastercard Incorporated (MA)

Mastercard Incorporated is a leading global payments technology company headquartered in Purchase, New York, USA. It operates one of the world’s largest electronic payment networks, connecting consumers, financial institutions, merchants, governments, and businesses worldwide. Mastercard enables electronic payments through credit, debit, and prepaid cards, and provides advanced services in cybersecurity, analytics, and digital payment innovation. The company is a key player in the global shift toward cashless and contactless payments, with a strong focus on fintech integration and secure transaction processing.
